# Every storm repo

Renamed on 11 Oct 2026 to `tn10-storm-YYYY-MM-DD` (+ suffix). Old names redirect; old names are never reused. Visibility as of 11 Oct 2026. Private repos stay 404 for the public; each has a public clean copy or is a private original of one.

**Columns (since 13 Nov 2026).** *Class* is what a repo is (RUN, PRE-RUN, REHEARSAL, PLAN, COMPANION, DUP). *Status* is how it ended: `done`, `done (…)` with what was not met, `failed`, `void` (no usable data), `not run`, `plan`, or `n/a` for companions and duplicates. *Outcome* and *Lesson* are one line each, taken from the run's own files or from tn-master's day pages in [history/storms](../history/storms/README.md) (marked "day page", one lesson per day, so rows of the same day share it); "not recorded" means we did not write one down. *FINDINGS* are item numbers in [FINDINGS.md](../FINDINGS.md); item 22 covers the whole pre-run 1–20 series. The longer text in *What* and *Note* is kept as it was; where a date cell held more than a date, it moved into *Note*. Row 31 was never used.
