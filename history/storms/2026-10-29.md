## Deviations

- The state handoff was not copied from the mini, so step 1 ran on the mini sender alone.
- Box public lanes (muon, boson) were partial: they sent only 19:24-19:33Z and nothing in step 3; boson 55 inclusions from 5,314 attempts.
- Box clock about 0.9 s off.
- No second in the counted minutes had all four senders on at once.
