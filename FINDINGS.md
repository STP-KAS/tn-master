# FINDINGS

Numbered, each with a label (**A** measured on TN10, **B** same code but not tested elsewhere, **C** opinion, **upstream** = someone else's work, not tested by us) and its source. Testnet-10 only.

1. **The mass ceiling.** A full block holds about 304-308 simple payments, so about **3,040 to 3,080 payments per second** at 10 blocks per second. Submitting faster only fills mempools. **A.** [what-limits-tx-rate](https://github.com/STP-KAS/what-limits-tx-rate), [tn10-storm-2026-10-07](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-10-07).
2. **"Processed" overstates unique throughput:** 267.2/s unique selected-chain accepted vs 357.2/s Processed in the same window. **A.** [round 7](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-26-r7).
3. **Duplicates keep unique accepted near 2,000.** On 9 Oct, 48-49% of transaction slots in blocks were duplicates. **A.** [tn10-storm-2026-10-09](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-10-09).
4. **Mempool cap panic** at `--ram-scale=0.1` (`100001 > 100000`); feerate eviction works (30,298 evictions). **A.** [round 1, F1](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-25-r1/blob/main/findings/F1-mempool-cap-panic.md).
5. **The mempool is not saved on restart** (round 1, F2). **A.** [F2](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-25-r1/blob/main/findings/F2-mempool-not-persisted.md). **The public indexer froze** from 25 Sep 21:55 CEST (19:55Z) for at least 3 days 15 hours. **A.** [indexer-stall](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-25-indexer-stall), [explorer-check](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-26-explorer-check).
6. **Pruning disk bursts and node freezes.** Pruning can take up to about 16 GB in 30 min; nodes froze (DAA still, sink lag up to about 16 min) several times, including n2 on 11 Oct 11:08-11:25Z. **A.** [MASTER](history/MASTER.md), [tn10-storm-2026-10-11 WRITE-UP](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-10-11/blob/main/logs/WRITE-UP.md).
7. **Kaspa Pulse's node-dead rule.** On 9-10 Oct our senders kept "sending" about 1 million per minute at a dead node while the chain showed only base load; **Kaspa Pulse** spotted it in his outside count. Rule since then: RPC failing, not synced, lag over 60 s, or DAA still for 30 s means pause and fail over; a failed submit is a reject, never "sent". **A** (his count) . [credits/KASPA-PULSE.md](credits/KASPA-PULSE.md), [his notes](credits/kaspa-pulse/2026-10-09-night/tn10-2026-10-09-2125-0535-notes.txt).
8. **Count soon after the run (Kaspa Pulse).** On non-archival nodes, older blocks are pruned; the chain count must run right after END. **A.** [credits/KASPA-PULSE.md](credits/KASPA-PULSE.md) input 14; COUNT step in every plan since 10 Oct.
9. **Base load about 56 tx/s (Kaspa Pulse).** TN10 carries about 56 tx/s without us; every network count here includes it (55 tx/s measured on the night of 9-10 Oct). **A** (his figure). [METRICS.md](METRICS.md).

11. **Stale mempool after IBD (upstream fix in progress).** A node that catches up by IBD can keep transactions that synced blocks already accepted in its mempool for up to 24 h, inputs already spent. Mostly non-mining nodes; a mining node clears them on its next block template. This can inflate public nodes' mempool sizes after restarts during storms. Fix proposed in [rusty-kaspa PR #1147](https://github.com/kaspanet/rusty-kaspa/pull/1147) (fixes #1146, open as of 11 Oct 2026): revalidate the whole mempool when IBD completes. **Label: upstream, not tested by us.** Link only, no comments on kaspanet repos.
12. **Kaspa Stream shows different numbers on mobile iOS than on desktop Chrome** (TPS, covenants and other counters do not match). **Observed by stp; screenshots pending.** Background that may explain it ([tn10-storm-2026-10-06, "API up, Kaspa.stream is a different path"](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-10-06#api-up-kaspastream-is-a-different-path)): the page's counters come over socket.io from four separate socket servers; from the desk, at the same minute, three answered 403 and one 503, and a dead or refused socket shows the charts at zero or stuck. Chart TPS is accepted-transaction indexing, which lags. **Not sure / open for debate** until screenshots of the same minute are compared. Also: kaspa.stream showed the same frozen transaction list as api-tn10 on 26 Sep ([explorer-check](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-26-explorer-check)).
13. **Fees under congestion.** Under a 57-minute overload the minimum fee waited about 7 s at the median and up to 105 s; 10× the minimum always confirmed in 3.7 s or less; 100× bought nothing more. A 150× fee on small coins raised storage mass and lowered throughput. **A.** [overload summary](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-25-r1/blob/main/findings/overload-30m-summary.md), [round 5](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-09-26-r5).
14. **Mass sender stalls follow box pauses (11 Oct).** Four of five mass stalls came right after a 3-14 minute gap in the bot's own logs. **Not sure / open for debate.** [WRITE-UP](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-10-11/blob/main/logs/WRITE-UP.md).

Evidence already in [ADVICE items 1, 2, 3, 5, 8](ADVICE-TO-DEVELOPERS.md#hypothetical-scenario-mint-rush-after-smart-contracts-stp-11-oct-2026).
